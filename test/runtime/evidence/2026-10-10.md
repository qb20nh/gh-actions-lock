## Remaining gate

The service must reject syntactically malformed locks before executing the
sentinel. Re-run that negative control after the upstream fix and attach its
lock-specific error and absence of execution. Positive `$/` binding and pinned
downloads are established for this recorded deployment; strict runtime
malformed-lock handling is not. Generated workspace actions remain outside the
supported static model under [#128](https://github.com/github/gh-actions-lock/issues/128).

A subsequent [workaround assessment](2026-10-10-workarounds.md) proves that an
action-free validator job can reject malformed locks and keep its dependent
consumer unexecuted. It also demonstrates that a pinned parent alone does not
freeze mutable remote children. This provides a local mitigation path while the
original service rejection gate remains open.
